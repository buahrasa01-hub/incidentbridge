# IncidentBridge — Privacy Policy

*Effective date: 10 November 2026. Developer: Buahrasa Labs. Contact: buahrasa01@gmail.com*

## What IncidentBridge does

IncidentBridge is an Atlassian Forge app for Jira. It compares Jira issue summaries with their workflow status category and shows consistency findings, with optional one-click, human-confirmed fixes, inside Jira.

## Data the app accesses

The app accesses Jira issues (including issue summaries and statuses) and Jira Service Management requests through Atlassian's APIs, acting as the user who is viewing or running it (asUser). This processing happens entirely inside Atlassian's Forge platform.

## Data storage and transfer

- The developer operates no servers for this app and receives no data from it.
- The app uses no external storage, no third-party services, no analytics, and no AI/LLM services. No data leaves Atlassian's platform.
- The app does not store personal data in its own storage. Any change it makes (for example, an issue transition) is a normal Jira change performed inside your Atlassian site, at your explicit click.

## Your choices

Uninstalling the app from your Atlassian site stops all access immediately. Questions: buahrasa01@gmail.com.
